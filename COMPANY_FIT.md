# Hardware-Box — Company and Team Fit

**Snapshot:** 2026-11-13

## Project thesis

A toolkit that inspects engineering files such as CAD meshes and PCB records, checks their structure and reports possible design issues.

These matches are prospective employer/skills hypotheses, not evidence of adoption, endorsement, partnership or hiring interest.

## ABB Robotics

**Relevant public technical areas:** Industrial robotics hardware and engineering verification.

**Potential role families:** ML Engineer, Research Engineer, Software Engineer, Systems Engineer, Data Engineer or Robotics Engineer, depending on current openings.

**Portfolio evidence to build:** Python toolkit and Streamlit interface for read-only inspection of STL/3MF meshes, tolerance stacks, BOM records, native KiCad files and project integrity. It produces advisory engineering checks; it is not a CAD-kernel replacement, manufacturing approval or certification.
